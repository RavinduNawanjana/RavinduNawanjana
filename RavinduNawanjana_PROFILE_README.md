I work at the intersection of **environmental economics, climate finance, sustainable finance, financial intermediation, carbon markets, natural capital and energy transition**, with a particular interest in emerging markets, the G77 and China, Small Island Developing States and Least Developed Countries.

My research portfolio combines **mixed methods, econometrics, reproducible statistical analysis and applied computational modelling**. I use code to make assumptions, evidence boundaries, diagnostics and uncertainty visible rather than treating repositories as presentation-only project pages.

## Research focus

- Multilateral climate funds, blended finance and institutional architecture
- Green finance, stock exchanges and financial intermediation
- Carbon markets, AFOLU and regenerative agriculture
- Energy economics and clean-technology adoption
- Natural capital, biodiversity finance and climate policy
- Reproducible research for emerging-market environmental economics

## Degree research

### Multilateral climate finance architecture · MSc research

**Practitioner perspectives on institutional collaboration and multilateral climate funds in the Group of 77 and China**

Mixed-methods research on institutional collaboration, blended finance deployment, policy and regulatory alignment, transparency and climate-finance architecture. The public computational companion uses **R + Quarto** for reproducible statistical analysis and research reporting.

[![Repository](https://img.shields.io/badge/Repository-multilateral--climate--finance--architecture-2f6d4f?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/RavinduNawanjana/multilateral-climate-finance-architecture)

### Market intermediaries and the green transition · BSc research

**Evidence on Stock Exchanges and Bank Green Finance Adoption in Emerging Markets**

R-first reproducible analysis of the associated Figshare survey and SPSS files. The repository uses **R + Quarto** as the primary statistical workflow, **Python/Jupyter** as an independent audit layer, **SPSS** as preserved source evidence, and **GitHub Actions** for clean-environment verification.

[![Repository](https://img.shields.io/badge/Repository-stock--exchange--intermediation--green--finance-0b3d8f?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/RavinduNawanjana/stock-exchange-intermediation-green-finance)
